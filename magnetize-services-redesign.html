Regulated categories

How do search marketing services change in regulated categories?

Each platform sets its own rules for sensitive categories, and these rules change frequently. Some prohibit ads entirely, while others quietly restrict campaign data, causing performance to decline even when ads appear approved.

PPC

Certification & verification

Online pharmacies must obtain Google certification before running ads, and UK financial services advertisers require verification. Many medical terms are restricted, and certain audience features are unavailable in sensitive categories.

SEO

Higher content standards

Search engines apply higher standards to health and finance content. Pages must feature qualified authors, credible sources, and verifiable claims.

ChatGPT Ads

Case-by-case review

OpenAI currently restricts or reviews most healthcare and financial services advertising on a case-by-case basis. For many regulated brands, this channel is not yet available.

Paid social

Extra rules & data limits

Meta enforces additional rules for health and wellness ads, including age targeting and restrictions on certain claims. It may also limit the conversion data available to health brands for campaign optimisation.

What do we do about it?

  1. 01

    We check the rules before you spend, platform by platform, for your exact category.

  2. 02

    We replace lost signals with your own data. Where audience features are restricted, offline conversions from your CRM still show the platforms who convert.

  3. 03

    We keep ads and content defensible. No unsupported “best” or “leading” claims, and no promises regulators would question.

  4. 04

    We watch for quiet failures. If a platform starts blocking data, we catch it within days, not a month later in your reports.

Questions

Search marketing services FAQs

What’s included in search marketing services?
Search marketing services cover channels that put a business in front of buyers when they search: PPC (paid search), SEO (organic search), and increasingly, ads inside AI platforms such as ChatGPT. Many agencies, including Magnetize, also run paid social to reach buyers before they search.
Can I start with just one service?
Yes. Many clients start with PPC or SEO and add other channels once the first works. We’ll recommend where to start based on your goals, budget, and category.
How long does each service take to show results?
PPC can deliver enquiries within weeks of launch. SEO builds over months because rankings take time to earn. ChatGPT Ads and paid social fall in between, depending on your category and how much buyers need to learn about you.
What are ChatGPT Ads, and how are they different from Google Ads?
ChatGPT Ads are paid placements that appear alongside answers inside ChatGPT, while buyers are researching and comparing options. Google Ads appear in search results after someone types a query. We run both from the same conversion and margin data, so they work as one plan.
Do you work with in-house marketing teams?
Yes. Alongside fully managed accounts, we offer an embedded specialist who works alongside your team, or support to bring your search marketing in-house.
Are you a full-service agency?
No. We do search and paid media only: no web builds, brand campaigns, or PR. That focus is deliberate.
